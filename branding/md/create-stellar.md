**The 1.21.1 update is here. Fresh start on NeoForge with Create 6, a brand new quest book, and a whole custom mod full of pack-only stuff.**

Create Stellar is a modpack all about automation, exploring space, and more.

## Your adventure starts here!

One of the main features of this pack is its progression system. The game consists of 5 main stages, and some sub-stages, which will change how you progress through the game.

These stages will help you get through all the different mods and once you're done you will have mastered almost every mod in the pack. Especially Create!

This modpack is perfect for both experts and people who have never played with the Create mod before! It has a very well explained introduction to the mods at the start, then as you progress everything becomes harder.

Quests run on Tenet now, 600+ of them across the whole progression. All the custom items, liquids, planet cobblegens, recipes, and the dark interface come from the companion mod that ships with the pack, so there is nothing extra to download or set up.
