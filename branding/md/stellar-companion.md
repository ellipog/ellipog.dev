**The stuff that makes Create Stellar Create Stellar.**

This is the pack's own mod. Its items, its liquids, the planet cobblegens, a few hundred recipes, and the dark interface. One mod built for exactly one modpack. Grab the pack, not this on its own.

## What's inside

- **25 items.** 11 mechanism lines plus their unfinished versions, and the steel rod, zinc hand, and solid fuel clump.
- **19 liquids.** Molten metals, gems, oils, and fuel. All of them have buckets.
- **9 planet cobblegens.** Lava meets water over moon rock, Mars rock, Mercury, Venus, or Glacio rock and you get the local stone instead. Every one of them has its own EMI page.
- **The pack's recipes.** Around 280 of them. Gated machines, alloys, melting, and the little shared tags nothing else adds.
- **The interface skin.** Dark mode GUI, built right in. No resource pack to turn on.

No config file. The cobblegen list is fixed because it only serves this pack.

It needs the pack's other mods, and it says so: every dependency is declared, so if something is missing the loader tells you exactly what.
